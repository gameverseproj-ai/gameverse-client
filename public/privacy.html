GameVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

GameVerse is a browser game. This page explains what data the game stores and why.

What we collect

  • Game progress: points, rewards, collections, language and music preferences.
  • Device identifier: a random ID generated in your browser so guest progress can be saved without an account.
  • Account identity (only if you sign in): your Google account ID, name and email, or your Telegram ID, name and username. We use it solely to link your progress to you across devices.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not read your Google contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data beyond basic profile and email.

Storage and retention

Progress and identity data are stored on our servers for as long as your account exists. Session tokens are stored in your browser's local storage.

Your choices

You can keep playing as a guest without signing in. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the address below.
